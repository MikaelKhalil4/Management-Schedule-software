# 🦊Smart Business Scheduling & Management System

> **🎓 open-source – free to use. Contact for deployment assistance.**



A full-featured scheduling and management system. It was developed with a focus on performance, flexibility, and solving real-world scheduling problems in business operations.

📺 **Demo Video**: [Meet the Client Management and Schedule software! - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPkEBWAOwBw&feature=youtu.be))

---

## ⚙️ Key Features (Technical Overview)

### 🔒 Roles And Permissions
- Role-based access for admin, employees, and clients
- Custom permissions per role

### 🗓️ Smart Appointment Scheduler
- 7-day calendar view with drag-and-drop UI
- Multi-column view by employee availability
- Auto-detects available client packages and applies sessions
- Direct payment from within the schedule
- Integrated reminders beside each day's schedule

### 👥 Client Management
- Full client profiles with customizable fields
- Parent-child relationship support (e.g., families, guardians)
- History of visits, payments, offers, and package usage

### 📦 Package & Membership System
- Sell services in bundles (by sessions or days)
- Track session usage and remaining balances
- Supports standalone memberships (e.g., 10-session haircut plan)

### 💰 Sales & Income Analytics
- Chart income by time, product, service, or employee
- Detailed transaction history
- Employee-linked sales & undo functionality
